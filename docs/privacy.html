Iron Night — Privacy policy

Iron Night is a single-player game by Peter Monev. This page says what the game does with data. Last updated 21 September 2026.

What the game stores

Your progress (depot points, unlocked tanks and commanders, medals, records, settings) is stored only on your device, in the game's own storage. It is not sent anywhere. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

What the game does not do

The game has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. It does not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, location, photos or any other personal data, and it does not read them.

Advertising

The game may show rewarded video ads (for example to double a night's score). Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier to measure and personalise ads, as described in Google's privacy policy. You can reset or limit the advertising identifier in your device settings. The game itself does not collect or store this identifier.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
