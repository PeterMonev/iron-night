Iron Night — Privacy policy

Iron Night is a single-player game by Peter Monev. This page says what the game does with data. Last updated 26 September 2026.

What the game stores

Your progress (depot points, crew experience, gold, premium service time, unlocked tanks, crews and commanders, medals, records, settings) is stored only on your device, in the game's own storage. It is not sent anywhere. Uninstalling the game deletes it, including gold you bought.

What the game does not do

The game has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. It does not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, location, photos or any other personal data, and it does not read them.

Advertising

The game offers rewarded video ads: you choose to watch one for a reward (for example a field repair or a little gold). No ad is shown without you asking for it. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier and other device information to deliver, measure and, with your consent, personalise ads, as described in Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game first asks for your consent through Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete the advertising identifier in your device settings. The game itself does not collect or store this identifier.

Purchases

Gold can be bought in the game's shop. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play, through Unity's In-App Purchasing library; the game never sees your payment details. It receives only a confirmation of the purchase and keeps its transaction number on your device, so that the same purchase is never counted twice. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
